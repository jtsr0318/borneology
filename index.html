Celebrating Borneo's Heritage

Discover the rich cultural tapestry of Sarawak through symbols that inspire our artisans: the majestic hornbill, the rare rafflesia, and the playful proboscis monkey.

Hornbill

The majestic hornbill, Sarawak's state bird, symbolizes strength and wisdom. Its elegant form inspires patterns in our textiles and wood carvings.

Rafflesia

The world's largest flower, native to Borneo's rainforests. Its unique beauty influences vibrant color palettes in our beaded jewelry.

Proboscis Monkey

Endemic to Borneo, this playful primate represents harmony with nature. Featured in storytelling through our woven baskets and crafts.
