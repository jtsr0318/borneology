🎉 Welcome to Seller Dashboard!

Congratulations on becoming a verified seller! This quick guide will help you get started with selling your handcrafted products on Borneology.

1 Access Your Seller Dashboard

Navigate to your Seller Dashboard from your profile menu or click the button below.

📦 Seller Dashboard → Manage all your products and orders

You can access it anytime from your profile dropdown menu.

2 Add Your First Product

In the Seller Dashboard, you'll find a product form. Here's how to add a product:

  • Product Name: Give your product a clear, descriptive name
  • Price: Set the price in Malaysian Ringgit (RM)
  • Category: Choose the appropriate category (Textile, Bag/Weave, Wood Carving, Beads, or Other)
  • Stock Quantity: Enter how many items you have available
  • Description: Write a detailed description of your product
  • Product Image: Upload a high-quality photo (JPG/PNG, max 5MB)
💡 Tip: Use clear, well-lit photos that showcase your craftsmanship!

3 Manage Your Products

In the Products tab, you can:

  • View all your listed products
  • Edit product details (click "Edit" button)
  • Update stock quantities
  • Delete products that are no longer available

Keep your product information up-to-date to ensure smooth transactions.

4 Handle Orders

When customers purchase your products, you'll see them in the Orders tab:

  • Pending: New orders waiting for your confirmation
  • Processing: Orders you're preparing to ship
  • Shipped: Orders that have been sent out
  • Delivered: Completed orders
📦 Update order status as you process and ship items

5 Track Your Analytics

The Analytics tab shows you:

  • Total number of products listed
  • Total orders received
  • Total revenue generated
  • Pending orders count
  • Product category distribution

Use this data to understand your sales performance and plan your inventory.

6 Best Practices

  • Respond to orders promptly
  • Keep accurate stock counts
  • Upload high-quality product images
  • Write detailed, honest product descriptions
  • Update order status regularly
  • Maintain good communication with buyers
